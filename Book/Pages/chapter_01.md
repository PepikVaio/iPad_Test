# Chapter 1

"Who are you?" murmured a woman to a younger girl sitting next to her hospital bed.

"I am your best friend. Remember? Ema. The girl you met at work." said Ema with a bittersweet tone at her voice.

"No, I don't have any memories of you. Why did the doctors let you in my room? Why do I hear these words in my head now? "Ahoj, Pavlínko."

"Yes, yes. You're name is Paula. In Czech, I nicknamed you Pavlínka. I am Czech. I've taught you a couple of words. We’ve been really close." Ema held a tear back.

"Pavlínka. Yeah, my name. And who am I, tell me, my friend as you say so?" Paula tilted her head and glanced at Ema.

"Well, there's a lot to tell you. You've been my best friend for a long time. My first memory of you is us going out for a lunch. I was asking you questions why you came to Prague. I really liked you from the first day. It's like we clicked." smiled Ema.

"I must have been likeable and social person."

"Yeah, your life has been changing a lot throughout the years."

"Go on, you Ema from Czech."

"Ok, so when you were a lot younger, you married a guy and opened a clothes shop. You were not really happy. You struggled a lot in this period of life."

"Oh, why did I marry this guy?"

"I think you were forced to do it."

"I wouldn't marry a man because my parents would say so. Nah."

"Maybe now, but back then you thought differently. You didn't know anything else than that little town you lived in."

"I lived in a little town? Me? I remember that I saw huge towers, people rushing to work in the morning, buses and trams ringing, loud chatter. I must have lived in a city like New York.

Wow. You help me remember my life pretty well. Wait. Do I have a child?"

"Yes, you do, a small boy named Lauren."

"What's our surname? What's my nationality? Where do I come from?"

"Your surname is Heinfeld. You come from a country at war now. I think I can show you on TV." Ema turns on the TV and there are TV reports of scenes of wars, death and disturbance.

"Oh no. My home country is in flames. Do I have a family? Where's my son?"

"He is sitting at the nurse's room. I didn’t know how would he comprehend that you don't remember him."

"Let Lauren in. I wanna see him- what he looks like and what he is like."

Lauren comes in rushing to his momma.

"Mommy, I am so happy to see you."

"Hi Lauren. I've missed you."

"Lauren, can you tell mommy how was your school today?"

"Oh mommy. I got As at school today."

"Oh, my clever boy. I've known that you are awesome in every way."

Ema took Lauren back to nurse's room.

"Oh, Ema. I don't remember anything about Lauren. It's as if he never existed." Paula’s face turned red and tears went streaming down her face.

"Ema, I am so messed up. I don't know anything about my life, about Lauren. About my husband. Wait, is there a husband in my life?"

"Well, no."

"Lauren doesn't have a father?"

"There's no one waiting for me at home to hug me and to talk with about my day."

"I am sorry but that's true."

Paula didn't say anything just the tears kept running and falling.

"Where's my family? Are they still alive? You said my country was at war."

"Your family has been displaced all across the continent. Look at your phone and call mom. She's gonna tell you more about you. I'll be back tomorrow and I'll take Lauren to his nanny."

"Who's his nanny?"

"Look at your phone and call her. She's normal. You booked the nanny for a month in advance. You were supposed to work a lot of overtime in a different city before Christmas. You got home very very late every day. Lauren didn't see you for a whole month, just the nanny."

"Am I rich? What is my occupation?"

"Yes, you are quite a rich business woman. You sell your own cosmetic products. You have employees that you manage."

"Oh, how much do I make?"

"Usually a million yearly."

"Wow. I forgot how to do the business."

"Well, Pavlínko. You're gonna remember. I brought you some snacks and photos to help you remember your life better."

"Thank you. I'll check them."

"Ahoj, Pavlínko. Mám tě ráda." waved and sent a kiss to Paula.

"Ahoj, Emička. Mám tě ráda. Oh, I can really speak Czech a bit."

Ema went home and reminisced about her almost lost friendship. During her ride on a bus, she looked at the smiley photos of her and Paula and Lauren. She called her boyfriend:

"Oh, Jack. She doesn't remember anything. I've lost a friend of my life.

It hurts so much." Ema was sobbing and hang up the phone call.

Ema thought to herself: Now, Paula is a woman with no past. I gotta call her family members and reconnect them.

The next afternoon Ema visited Paula at her hospital room feeling as if her heart was breaking. Losing a best friend hurts.

"Everybody hurts sometimes." whispered Ema under her breath.

She suppressed the grief and sadness and entered her friend's room.

"Ahoj, Pavlínko. Jak se máš?" smiled Ema with tremble in her voice.

"Ahoj, Ema. Dobře. Děkuji." said Paula with a thick accent.

"I've just read this book about the Czech Republic. To find out more about your culture. There's one page on Czech basics. I've learned to say I love you and the word love in Czech."

"Oh cool. How do you say it?" grinned Ema.

"So love is láska, right? And I love you is Miluji tě."

"Yeah, but we say Miluji tě to a romantic partner and for friends and family we use Mám tě ráda. (i like you)." Ema took the advantage of being a native speaker and corrected her friend.

"Uh huh. So, I love you to you as a friend would be Mám tě ráda. Maaam tie raaada. Rrrr. I like the way Czech sounds - quite funny." Paula couldn't hold back her laughter.

Ema changed the topic:

"What's new, Pavlínko?"

"The doctors told me that my memory should be gradually coming back and I need more information, photos and phonecalls. I should be discharged in two weeks. What did you bring me today, Emička?"

"Oh good Lord, your memory should come back. Well, more photos.

I am in them with Jack, you and Lauren back in Prague. It was winter 2023. I remember how Lauren was in awe of Prague's historical monuments and wanted to cross the Charles bridge. Look."

"Lauren is such a beautiful child. I am proud that he’s mine. How is he?" glanced Paula at Ema.

"Well, he has been missing you for a long time now. He cries a lot but your mom promised to keep an eye on him and come to your place. Your mum told me she would visit you with Lauren in 3 days. She's not yet able to call you, Paula. She's very upset with the accident that happened to you a month ago."

"A month ago?" Paula raised her eyebrows and almost lost consciousness.

"Yeah, one month ago. You were driving your car going from work. You had a microsleep and crashed. You survived but were in a comma and woke up three days ago. We're all happy to have you back. We were really worried that we had lost you."

"This is terrible to hear. I guess I'll need a therapy. Do they provide this service here? I gotta check with the nurses. Well, what I wanted to add, I tried to call my father, he didn't pick up. Next person I called was the nanny and she actually picked up the phone. She said how happy she was to hear me and that I am well now. She repeated her name again... Suzanne Leaster. She studies university and is a certified nanny. I had a good choice. Huh."

"I'll go to nurse's room and ask her about the therapy. This is a good step to get back to your normal life as soon as possible. I appreciate you want to take this step."

"Thank you, Emička."

Next day in the hospital room, there appears Paula's ex boyfriend. He came to say a friendly hello.

"Good morning, Paula. It's nice to see you after long time. I hope you're well now."

"Oh, this voice reminds me of somebody who broke my heart. This must be you, Daniel. What are you doing here? I feel like I hated you."

"I'd like to just say hi. After what I heard had happened to you, I wanted to check on you. I don't know if you remember, but even after we broke up, we still hung out, sometimes."

"No, I don't feel like that's what I'd have done. I don't like you now."

"I am telling the truth."

"I don't believe you. My senses tell me that you treated me terribly. I don't know if I want to be in your presence anymore. You must have done something terrible to me."

"I didn't do anything terrible to you. Trust me."

"Haha. Good joke. I feel bad with you. Leave please."

"No, Paula. I want to talk to you."

"Ok, say what you want and then leave."

"Alright. I still love you and I want you back."

"Daniel, you've hurt me terribly. I don't know what to do now. Give me some time. I see I have a contact listed as Daniel Ress, is that you?"

"Yes. I'll call you."

Paula just rolled her eyes and said: “Ok, fine. Mister-break-my-heart-and-disappear-for-years. Bye. I want to rest now.”

Daniel left the room disappointed.

That same day, two doctors came to inform Paula about her progress.

"Hello, Ms. Heinfeld, how are you doing these days? Anything new that you remembered today? Any visitor?"

"Yeah, today my ex boyfriend Daniel visited me. I just looked at him and instantly felt hated and anger. Then I heard his voice and realized who he was. It wasn't the best encounter today. I feel like my memory's coming back more and more easily. I still have strong migraines and panic attacks, but other than that I am fine."

"Nice to hear you remember the people easier. We'll still prescribe you antidepressants and some pills for migraines, and, Ms. Heinfeld, please expect that you'll be going to psychologist and psychiatrist for easier recovery and dealing with panic attacks. This is all natural what you've been going through. You’ve been doing a fast progress. We're very glad to see you doing well. We're gonna send you to psychiatrist next week. You'll be discharged after psychiatrist appointment, if all goes well."

"So, I'll get medicine for the migraines, go to psychiatrist and psychologist. And next week, I'll be discharged. Did I get it, right?"

"Yes, Ms. Heinfeld. Your short-term memory is working as it should. Now, read some books and study. Train your memory and enjoy your dinner. Bye."

Paula was in such a cheerful mood that she called Ema to tell it all to her.

"Ahoj, Emička. I have news."

"Ahoj, Pavlínko. Yes? Tell me."

"Ema, I will probably be discharged next week after my psychiatrist appointment.

Daniel Ress, Mr-Break-My-Heart-And-Disappear-For-Years. This asshole still loves me. Damn it. Huh. What am I thinking? I need somebody in my life. He’s hot. Oh shit, I think I am falling in love. But then he really hurt me and feel terrible next to him, but could this change? I’ll call him today." Paula called Daniel’s number, her heart was beating fast and cheeks blushed in shame.

Daniel picked up: “Oh, hi, Paula. I wondered if you’d call. How’s it going?”

“Hello, Daniel. I’ve been doing well for a while. I think I’d like to meet... sorry to hear you. How you were and stuff.” Paula hung up immediately.

“What the fuck was I thinking to rekindle the old flame. Damn it.” sounded through Paula’s head.

“Ok, you know what, you stupid hot-headed woman, call him again and tell him what you feel. Fuck it, it’ll be fine.”

Paula called back again.

“Daniel, I think I need to see you.. There’s something in me that I can’t ignore. I, I, I, think I have feelings for you.”

Daniel went: “Oh, holy cow. Ok, let’s meet next week at a café near the town square?”

“Yeah, please. I’ll call you when I am discharged. Bye, Dan.”

“Bye, Paula.”

The day Paula and Daniel met....

“Fuck, Ema. What am I supposed to wear to a date with my ex that I know but don’t?”

“Wear something in which you’d feel comfortable. Send me some pics. I’ll give you some opinions.”
